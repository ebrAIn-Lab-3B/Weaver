## ebrAIn.lab Weaver 0.1.21

Read ordinary Markdown as a rendered document, including Mermaid diagrams, and begin writing ideation and requirement documents without an agent.

### What you get

- `weaver-0.1.21.vsix` for VS Code 1.90 or later.
- `weaver.js`, the command-line tool, for Node 20 or later.

### What is new

- Ordinary Markdown and task files open in the rendered Weaver document view by default, with an action to open their source. A task also offers a link to its requirement. Requirement documents keep their existing view. See [REQ-021](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/docs/requirements/REQ-021-render-ordinary-markdown.md) and the [preview example](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/examples/markdown-preview.md).
- Mermaid diagrams render locally. Invalid diagrams leave their source visible with an explanation.
- A single-line heading or paragraph can be edited from the rendered view. Other content can be edited in the source editor.
- Commands create local ideation and requirement files after showing their contents and destination. An ideation document can be linked from a requirement. See [REQ-022](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/docs/requirements/REQ-022-human-document-authoring.md), the [getting-started guide](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/docs/onboarding/getting-started.md), and the [proposed design](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/docs/decisions/DEC-027-markdown-viewing-and-authoring.md).

### Not yet

- A full block editor, slash commands, and direct editing of multiline blocks are not available.
- Requirement ID allocation is not atomic across concurrent processes.
- The design decision and the complete authoring and round-trip flows still require review and verification. Work is tracked in [TASK-115](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/docs/tasks/TASK-115.md), [TASK-118](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/docs/tasks/TASK-118.md), [TASK-119](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/docs/tasks/TASK-119.md), [TASK-120](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/docs/tasks/TASK-120.md), and [TASK-121](https://github.com/ebrAIn-Lab-3B/Weaver/blob/v0.1.21/docs/tasks/TASK-121.md).
- Publishing to the VS Code Marketplace or Open VSX.
